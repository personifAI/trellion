For organisations that cannot meet that condition, **PersonifAI LLC offers

This page describes what such a licence covers. It is not itself a licence:

## What a commercial licence covers

- **Library use without AGPL obligations.** Import, link, embed or extend

  offered over a network.
- **Support and updates** as set out in the agreement.

## What it does not change

- The AGPL source of the open part remains available to everyone,
  commercial licensees included. A commercial licence adds rights; it
  removes none.
- Contributions to the open part are accepted under the project CLA
  (`CLA.md`), which is what allows PersonifAI to offer this licence over the
  whole codebase.

PersonifAI holds the copyright in Trellion and, through the CLA, the rights
